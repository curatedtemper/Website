PRIVACY POLICY

Version 1.3  ·  Effective August 2026  ·  curatedtemper.com

1. Who We Are

Curated Temper is an IP monitoring and enforcement support service. Contact: hello@curatedtemper.com.

2. What Data We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name and email address — collected at signup and waitlist registration.
  • Payment information — processed by Stripe. We do not store card details.
  • Work descriptions and URLs — information about your creative works for monitoring purposes.
  • Case communications — messages related to monitoring and enforcement decisions.

Automatically Collected

  • Basic server logs — IP address, browser type, pages visited.
  • Web fonts — our website loads typography from Google Fonts. Displaying these fonts requires your browser to request the font files directly from Google's servers, which may process your IP address as part of that request.

We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, session-recording tools, or other tracking cookies.

Creative Work Data

We do not store full-resolution copies of your creative works. We may store reference fingerprints, metadata, and monitoring data for operational purposes.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Sensitive personal data (health, religion, political views, etc.).
  • Data from persons under 18.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide our IP monitoring and enforcement support services.
  • To communicate about your account, cases, and updates.
  • To process payments via Stripe.
  • To send monthly monitoring reports.
  • To improve our service and detect fraud.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to deliver subscribed services.
  • Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, service improvement.
  • Legal obligation — record retention as required by law.
  • Consent — for optional communications, obtained explicitly.

5. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise these rights, contact hello@curatedtemper.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Stripe — payment processing, governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree — processes information submitted through our application form (such as name and email) in order to deliver it to us. Governed by Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) — delivers the typography used on our website; this may involve processing your IP address. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Email service providers — for service communications and monthly reports.

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data for commercial purposes.

7. International Data Transfers

Some of the providers listed above, including Stripe, Formspree, and Google, may process data on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where required by applicable law, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to help ensure your data remains protected.

8. Data Retention

  • Account and contact information — retained during your subscription and for a reasonable period after termination.
  • Case records and monitoring reports — retained for up to 3 years.
  • Payment records — retained as required by financial regulations.
  • Server logs — retained for a maximum of 90 days.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, port, or object to processing of your data. Contact hello@curatedtemper.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. If you are located in the United Kingdom, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). These rights exist independently of, and in addition to, contacting us directly.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. No method of internet transmission is completely secure.

11. Children

Our services are not directed at persons under 18. Contact us immediately at hello@curatedtemper.com if you believe we have collected data from a minor.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy and will notify users via email or through our website.

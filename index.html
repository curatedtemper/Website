THE PROVENANCE
& EXPOSURE
ASSESSMENT

AI companies have scraped hundreds of billions of creative works to train their models. Yours may be among them — without your consent, without compensation.

We identify potential unauthorized uses of your work across supported platforms and known training sources, document findings, and prepare recommended actions for your review. As copyright law continues to evolve across the US, UK, and EU in 2026, that documented evidence becomes the foundation of any future claim.

This service is rooted in a law degree specializing in data protection law and the implications of using personal data in AI training contexts — a rare area of focus at the intersection of privacy law and creative rights.

Training Exposure Check
We cross-reference your work against publicly available AI training datasets and tell you plainly what that can, and cannot, rule out. Honesty over false comfort.
Provenance Certification
We embed a dated, cryptographically verifiable declaration of ownership and non-consent directly into your work — evidence that exists independently of any single legal ruling. Some platforms strip this on upload, which is exactly why we monitor for it.
Style & Derivative Exposure
We test whether your visual or written voice can be reconstructed by current-generation AI systems — a strong signal of unauthorized training exposure.
Formal Written Report
A documented, timestamped audit report. Evidence for today. Foundation for tomorrow.

Why we exist

THE INTERNET
INDUSTRIALIZED
EXTRACTION.

Platforms were built to distribute. Then they were built to monetize. Independent creators — illustrators, photographers, designers, writers, musicians — were given reach but never given protection. Their work became infrastructure for other people's profits.

Then AI arrived, and the same extraction logic simply found a new gear — moving at a speed and scale no single creator could ever see, let alone stop. The name of this company is not decoration. Curated Temper means controlled intensity, directed with purpose — the exact quality independent creators have always brought to their work, and the exact quality that was missing from anyone defending it.

"Art deserves armour. So does the artist." That is not a tagline. It is the only fact Curated Temper was built to act on.

The Problem

Most creators discover their work has been used without permission by accident — if at all. By then the damage is done and the evidence is gone.

The Shift

AI and platform scale changed the equation permanently. What was once a manageable problem became systematic. Visibility is now the first line of defense.

Our Answer

Curated Temper exists to give independent creators everywhere — not only those large enough to have institutional infrastructure — the protection institutions take for granted. Precise. Intelligent. Worldwide. On your side.
